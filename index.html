Freezer Kitchen

Cook 12 freezer containers once. Mix the cubes into 30 meals for your one-year-old in 5–15 minutes.

How it works

No salt, no sugar. Everything cooked until soft.

  1. Cook each container's mix
  2. Cool it within 1 hour
  3. Freeze in ice cube trays
  4. Bag, label and date the cubes
  5. Mix about 2 cubes of each, heat until piping hot
  6. Cool and serve

The 12 containers

What goes in each one and how to prep it.

01

Green veg

Container

Inside

Peas, broccoli, green beans

How to prep

  1. Steam peas, broccoli florets and green beans for 5–6 minutes until soft.
  2. Chop small or mash lightly.

Cool within 1 hour · ice cube trays · bag, label & date · keeps 3 months

02

Root veg

Container

Inside

Carrot, parsnip, swede

How to prep

  1. Peel and dice carrot, parsnip and swede.
  2. Steam or boil for about 15 minutes until soft.
  3. Mash or chop into small pieces.

Cool within 1 hour · ice cube trays · bag, label & date · keeps 3 months

03

Orange mash

Container

Inside

Sweet potato, butternut squash

How to prep

  1. Roast sweet potato and butternut squash at 200 °C for about 40 minutes.
  2. Scoop out the flesh and mash.

Cool within 1 hour · ice cube trays · bag, label & date · keeps 3 months

04

Leafy greens

Container

Inside

Spinach, kale

How to prep

  1. Wilt spinach and kale in a pan with a splash of water for 2–3 minutes.
  2. Squeeze out the water and chop very fine.

Cool within 1 hour · ice cube trays · bag, label & date · keeps 3 months

05

Sunny veg

Container

Inside

Sweetcorn, peppers, courgette

How to prep

  1. Dice courgette and peppers and soften in a little oil for 8–10 minutes.
  2. Stir in the sweetcorn and mash lightly.

Cool within 1 hour · ice cube trays · bag, label & date · keeps 3 months

06

Red sauce

Container

Inside

Tomato, onion, garlic

How to prep

  1. Soften chopped onion and garlic in olive oil.
  2. Add no-salt tinned chopped tomatoes and simmer for 20 minutes.
  3. Blend smooth.

Cool within 1 hour · ice cube trays · bag, label & date · keeps 3 months

07

Cauli cheese

Container

Inside

Cauliflower, cheese sauce

How to prep

  1. Boil cauliflower until soft.
  2. Make a white sauce with butter, flour and milk, then stir in mild cheddar.
  3. Blend the cauliflower into the sauce.

Cool within 1 hour · ice cube trays · bag, label & date · keeps 3 months

08

Chicken

Container

Inside

Shredded chicken

How to prep

  1. Poach chicken thighs in water for about 20 minutes until cooked through.
  2. Shred very fine.

Cool within 1 hour · ice cube trays · bag, label & date · keeps 3 months

09

Beef mince

Container

Inside

Beef mince

How to prep

  1. Brown in a dry pan, breaking it up small, until no pink is left.
  2. Drain off the fat.

Cool within 1 hour · ice cube trays · bag, label & date · keeps 3 months

10

Turkey mince

Container

Inside

Turkey mince

How to prep

  1. Cook like the beef until no pink is left.
  2. Stir in a spoon of red sauce so it stays moist.

Cool within 1 hour · ice cube trays · bag, label & date · keeps 3 months

11

Beans & lentils

Container

Inside

Lentils, chickpeas, kidney & butter beans

How to prep

  1. Simmer red lentils for 15–20 minutes until soft.
  2. Rinse no-salt tinned chickpeas and beans.
  3. Mash everything together.

Cool within 1 hour · ice cube trays · bag, label & date · keeps 3 months

12

Fruit mix

Container

Inside

Apple, pear, mango, berries

How to prep

  1. Peel and chop apple and pear, then stew with a splash of water for 10 minutes.
  2. Stir in mango and berries and mash.

Cool within 1 hour · ice cube trays · bag, label & date · keeps 3 months

01

Chicken Korma

Chicken · 5–15 min

You need

8Chicken3Orange mashPinch of cuminPlain yoghurtFresh rice

Method

  1. Cook a small handful of rice for 10–12 minutes.
  2. Put 2 cubes each of chicken and orange mash in a pan with a splash of water, plus a pinch of cumin.
  3. Cover and heat for 5–7 minutes, stirring, until piping hot all the way through.
  4. Take off the heat and stir in a spoon of plain yoghurt.
  5. Let it cool before serving.

Safety

The few rules that matter.

Don't freeze

  • Cooked egg goes rubbery. Make it fresh.
  • Cucumber, lettuce, raw tomato go mushy. Serve fresh.
  • Potato chunks go grainy. Freeze as mash.
  • Yoghurt and soft cheese separate. Add fresh after heating.

Stay safe

  • Reheat until piping hot, then cool before serving.
  • Never refreeze food that has thawed.
  • Rice: cool within 1 hour, reheat only once.
  • No honey before age 1, no whole nuts.
  • Quarter grapes and cherry tomatoes.
  • No added salt or sugar. Use no-salt tinned beans.
